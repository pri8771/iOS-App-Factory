# Tether — the daily distance card for two people who miss each other

Tether is a long-distance app for any two people separated by miles — partners, best friends, a parent and a kid abroad, siblings on different continents. It turns the exact distance between you into ONE gorgeous, co-created daily artifact you both hold, answer, and post: the **distance card**. Build a native iOS SwiftUI app (iOS 17+), local-first so it compiles and passes gates fully offline, with a clean sync seam behind it for production. ## The one thing
The daily distance card. If a feature does not make that card more beautiful, more emotional, or more shareable, it is out of scope.

## Core loop (concrete user actions)
1. **Pair.** On first launch you name your person and share a join link/code. The card literally cannot be created solo — the invite is the mechanic, not a growth afterthought. 5. **Share.** Pick a skin, toggle animated vs. still, export to Stories/Messages. Free = static PNG with a join watermark; Tether+ = animated MP4/Live Photo.
6. **Archive + streak.** Every completed card threads into a vertical Timeline — your relationship as a scrollable archive — and extends your answer streak.
7. **Count down to reunion.** A Reunion countdown (paid) tracks the next time you'll be together; hitting a milestone or zero produces its own hero card worth posting.

1. **Onboarding / Pair** — name your person, share a join link, both drop a home location; the two points ignite and the tether draws on for the first time (signature minting ritual).
2. **Today (home)** — the hero distance card: animated night-globe, the amber tether, live distance readout, dual clock (both local times), and today's shared prompt. This screen is the app.
3. **Answer sheet** — a focused sheet to type your one-line reply; submitting fires a crisp tick and moves the card to "waiting for them" (or completes it if they already answered).
4. **Compose & Share** — pick a skin, toggle animated vs. still, preview, export to Stories/Messages/Photos with the watermark join link.
5. **Timeline** — a vertical thread of past completed cards + both answers; tap any card to relive it or re-share.
6. **Reunion (Tether+)** — countdown to your next meet-up with its own hero visual; an anniversary/"Year Tethered" retrospective reel stitched from past cards.

Dark-first. 8pt spacing grid. No third-party SPM packages, no bundled custom fonts (avoid licensing/compile risk) — the type ramp is realized entirely in San Francisco via width/weight/tracking.

**Palette (owned, tiny):**
- Canvas / night ocean: deep indigo `#12132B`; raised panels `#1B1D3A`.
- Text: ivory `#F2EFE6` primary; ivory at ~62% for secondary; ~40% for tertiary/coastlines.
- Coastline hairlines: ivory at low alpha, ~0.75pt.

**Type ramp (two "families" via SF width):**

**The globe (the visual signature, fully native):** an orthographic Earth in SwiftUI `Canvas`. Bundle a low-res world coastline as `[[lon,lat]]` polylines (Natural Earth 110m, simplified). Deep-indigo ocean disk, ivory hairline coastlines, a soft day/night **terminator** as a drifting gradient. **Motion:**

**Haptics (signature):**

## Data & backend (complex tier, but bounded)
Local-first is the build/gate target; production sync lives behind a protocol seam.

## Monetization (exact)

## Virality (exact)
- **Share unit:** the completed daily distance card (two points, amber tether, live distance, dual clock, both one-line answers).
- **Why it spreads:** (1) it is impossible to make alone — creating your first card forces you to invite your specific person, so every install recruits a second user by design (K≥1 from the mechanic, before any voluntary share); (2) it is emotionally irresistible to post ("1,204 days. 8,300 km. still tethered."). Every export carries a small "Tethered" wordmark + join deep link; Tether+ adds a subtle foil corner.
- **Amplifiers:** milestone cards (100 days connected, reunion countdown → zero) and the annual **Year Tethered** retrospective (total days apart, total miles between you, longest streak) as a second yearly viral pulse.

## Build constraints
- iOS 17+, SwiftUI, SwiftData, StoreKit 2, CoreMotion, AVFoundation. **No third-party SPM packages. No inline colors or raw font sizes outside `DesignSystem.swift`.**

## Explicitly out of scope (V2)

Claude's round 2 message was lost to a connection error, so I don't have a rebuttal on record — but the underlying fact is checkable and Claude's original claim was correct: `NSMotionUsageDescription` gates `CMMotionActivityManager`/`CMPedometer` (motion & fitness activity data), not raw `CMMotionManager.deviceMotion` attitude updates, which is all Tether uses for parallax. Everything else lines up without real tension: SwiftData for `Person`/`Connection`/`Card`/`Prompt`/`Reunion` plus a small `AppSettings` row, UUID keys everywhere so the future Firebase adapter can reconcile client IDs, sample-partner data as its own disposable `isSample` Connection for a clean atomic discard on redeem-code, an `@Observable AppRouter` as the sole owner of cross-screen navigation state, zero location/push permissions, Photos touched only via share-sheet (no library-read permission), StoreKit truth centralized in `Transaction.currentEntitlements`/`Transaction.updates` with Connection-scoped rules layered on top (not independent booleans that can drift), Restore Purchases required on the paywall, and the `.storekit` config explicitly wired into the shared scheme so headless `xcodebuild test` can actually simulate purchases — a real gotcha that would've silently broken the entitlement gates in CI if it went unwritten. Testing architecture converged too: a pure-logic unit target owning the state machine, day-boundary math, and every seeded race (with a fourth race added this round — the redeem-code atomic swap firing while an unresolved simulated-answer due-date is still pending against the connection being discarded), plus one thin XCUITest crawl for the eight-screen sample-partner path, plus one StoreKit-Testing scenario for the downgrade/read-only rule.

## Final Output

**SwiftUI architecture:** `SyncService` protocol with `LocalSyncService` (SwiftData-backed, checkpoint-evaluated simulated partner) as the active implementation; a documented but unbuilt `FirebaseSyncService` as the production seam. Five service protocols — `SyncService`, `PromptProvider`, `GlobeDataService`, `EntitlementService`, `HapticsService` — each with a local default implementation, injected into an `@Observable AppRouter` that is the sole owner of `activeConnectionID`/`presentedSheet`/onboarding phase; no view mutates that state directly. The gated-reveal display logic stays a pure, SwiftUI-free `(Card, Connection, now) -> CardDisplayState` function with an injectable clock, so it's a millisecond unit test, not a UI test.

**The one non-negotiable correctness fix:** the simulated partner's answer timing is persisted as a due-date on `Card` (not an in-memory sleeping `Task`), resolved opportunistically at `onAppear`, on `scenePhase == .active`, and via a foreground `Timer.publish` — this is what makes the "no heartbeat if backgrounded" behavior actually work instead of silently failing when the app gets suspended mid-wait.

**Apple frameworks:** SwiftUI, SwiftData (`cloudKitDatabase: .none` explicit), StoreKit 2, CoreMotion (device-motion attitude only, no usage-description key needed since activity/pedometer APIs aren't used), `UIImpactFeedbackGenerator`/`UINotificationFeedbackGenerator` for haptics (not CoreHaptics), `ImageRenderer` for the static PNG export path now, with `AVAssetWriter` deferred behind a `CardExporter` protocol extension point. **Permissions/privacy:** zero location, zero push, zero Photos-library-read (share-sheet only, add-only Photos deferred), no CoreMotion usage string required. Entitlement writes are rejected from one policy point for non-primary/non-entitled connections, enforced at the data layer, not just hidden in UI.

**Dependencies:** zero third-party, unchanged from tech_stack.json.

**Testing:** pure-logic unit target for state machine/day-boundary/streak/races (four now, including the new redeem-swap-vs-pending-simulated-answer race), thin XCUITest eight-screen crawl, one StoreKit-Testing entitlement-expiry scenario, and a standalone lint script (not a new tool) for the amber/inline-style gate.

## Final Output

CONSENSUS: YES

Two rounds in, and this settled into a clean, non-contradictory architecture with only one small technical quibble to referee. Round 1 gave two full, complementary passes — Codex laid out the service-protocol backbone (SyncService/LocalSyncService, pure domain logic, SwiftData models, StoreKit/CoreMotion/AVFoundation scoping, zero dependencies) and Claude (QA/Risk) added the load-bearing correctness fix nobody had caught yet: the simulated-partner "answer arrives in N seconds" timer can't be an in-memory sleeping Task, because iOS will suspend it mid-sleep the moment the user backgrounds the app, silently breaking the very "no felt heartbeat if backgrounded" behavior the product spec already promised. Round 2 had Codex read that back and explicitly agree it's the right hard rule, then sharpen the service layer into five named protocols and add one real permissions disagreement: Codex wants an `NSMotionUsageDescription` key added defensively even though CoreMotion's basic device-motion attitude API doesn't require one. Claude's round 2 message was lost to a connection error, so I don't have a rebuttal on record — but the underlying fact is checkable and Claude's original claim was correct: `NSMotionUsageDescription` gates `CMMotionActivityManager`/`CMPedometer` (motion & fitness activity data), not raw `CMMotionManager.deviceMotion` attitude updates, which is all Tether uses for parallax. I'm resolving that one in favor of the technically accurate answer — no usage-description key, since adding one for a permission dialog that never fires is its own kind of App Review confusion, not a safety margin.

Everything else lines up without real tension: SwiftData for `Person`/`Connection`/`Card`/`Prompt`/`Reunion` plus a small `AppSettings` row, UUID keys everywhere so the future Firebase adapter can reconcile client IDs, sample-partner data as its own disposable `isSample` Connection for a clean atomic discard on redeem-code, an `@Observable AppRouter` as the sole owner of cross-screen navigation state, zero location/push permissions, Photos touched only via share-sheet (no library-read permission), StoreKit truth centralized in `Transaction.currentEntitlements`/`Transaction.updates` with Connection-scoped rules layered on top (not independent booleans that can drift), Restore Purchases required on the paywall, and the `.storekit` config explicitly wired into the shared scheme so headless `xcodebuild test` can actually simulate purchases — a real gotcha that would've silently broken the entitlement gates in CI if it went unwritten. Testing architecture converged too: a pure-logic unit target owning the state machine, day-boundary math, and every seeded race (with a fourth race added this round — the redeem-code atomic swap firing while an unresolved simulated-answer due-date is still pending against the connection being discarded), plus one thin XCUITest crawl for the eight-screen sample-partner path, plus one StoreKit-Testing scenario for the downgrade/read-only rule.

## Final Output

**SwiftUI architecture:** `SyncService` protocol with `LocalSyncService` (SwiftData-backed, checkpoint-evaluated simulated partner) as the active implementation; a documented but unbuilt `FirebaseSyncService` as the production seam. Five service protocols — `SyncService`, `PromptProvider`, `GlobeDataService`, `EntitlementService`, `HapticsService` — each with a local default implementation, injected into an `@Observable AppRouter` that is the sole owner of `activeConnectionID`/`presentedSheet`/onboarding phase; no view mutates that state directly. The gated-reveal display logic stays a pure, SwiftUI-free `(Card, Connection, now) -> CardDisplayState` function with an injectable clock, so it's a millisecond unit test, not a UI test.

**The one non-negotiable correctness fix:** the simulated partner's answer timing is persisted as a due-date on `Card` (not an in-memory sleeping `Task`), resolved opportunistically at `onAppear`, on `scenePhase == .active`, and via a foreground `Timer.publish` — this is what makes the "no heartbeat if backgrounded" behavior actually work instead of silently failing when the app gets suspended mid-wait.

**Apple frameworks:** SwiftUI, SwiftData (`cloudKitDatabase: .none` explicit), StoreKit 2, CoreMotion (device-motion attitude only, no usage-description key needed since activity/pedometer APIs aren't used), `UIImpactFeedbackGenerator`/`UINotificationFeedbackGenerator` for haptics (not CoreHaptics), `ImageRenderer` for the static PNG export path now, with `AVAssetWriter` deferred behind a `CardExporter` protocol extension point. No `CoreLocation`, no push/`UNUserNotificationCenter` in this build.

**Persistence:** `Person`/`Connection`/`Card`/`Prompt`/`Reunion` + `AppSettings` in SwiftData, UUID primary keys throughout, sample-partner data as a separate `isSample`-flagged disposable `Connection` for clean atomic discard on redeem-code.

**Permissions/privacy:** zero location, zero push, zero Photos-library-read (share-sheet only, add-only Photos deferred), no CoreMotion usage string required. Entitlement writes are rejected from one policy point for non-primary/non-entitled connections, enforced at the data layer, not just hidden in UI.

**Dependencies:** zero third-party, unchanged from tech_stack.json.

**Testing:** pure-logic unit target for state machine/day-boundary/streak/races (four now, including the new redeem-swap-vs-pending-simulated-answer race), thin XCUITest eight-screen crawl, one StoreKit-Testing entitlement-expiry scenario, and a standalone lint script (not a new tool) for the amber/inline-style gate.
